    Get them on every website

    This is for Chrome, Brave or Edge on a computer. One download and 4 clicks.

    The extension puts a small chat room in the corner of every website you visit. The nine voices talk about what's actually on the page, and you can talk back. Everything stays on your computer.

    On a phone? Extensions don't work on most phones. Open this page on a computer to get it. The voices on this page work fine here.

    Download the extension
    1. Find the file you just downloaded and unzip it. On Windows: right-click it, then Extract All. On a Mac: double-click it.
    2. Open a new tab and go to chrome://extensions. In Brave it's brave://extensions, in Edge it's edge://extensions.
    3. Turn on Developer mode (a switch at the top right, or on the left in Edge).
    4. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder, the one called browser-has-thoughts-extension.

    That's it. Open any website and the chat appears in the bottom corner. Press quiet to hush them for an hour, or remove the extension from the same page any time. Works in Chrome, Brave and Edge on a computer. Not Firefox or Safari yet.
    Everything happens in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. Each thought comes with a receipt: what the browser actually said.
